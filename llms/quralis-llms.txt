## About

QurAlis calls its team "neuro pioneers." The company was founded by neurodegenerative biologists
from Harvard Medical School and Harvard University to translate the discovery that different
disease drivers cause ALS into precision therapies, in the way oncology moved to targeted
treatment. Its scientific foundation is TDP-43 pathology: co-founder Kevin Eggan's lab showed in
2019 that loss of normal TDP-43 function drives a significant decrease in STATHMIN-2 (STMN2)
expression and an impairment in neuronal repair that can be rescued by restoring STMN2 levels
(published in Nature Neuroscience). TDP-43 pathology is present in nearly all ALS patients, roughly
50% of FTD patients, about a third of Alzheimer's disease patients and up to 7% of Parkinson's
disease patients.

## Pipeline

- **QRL-201** — a first-in-class molecule for ALS that aims to restore STMN2 expression. In the
  ANQUR clinical trial; QurAlis reported effects on disease progression and target engagement in
  February 2026.
- **QRL-101** — a potentially best-in-class selective Kv7.2/7.3 ion channel opener for
  hyperexcitability-induced disease progression in ALS, and for epilepsy and pain. Signal of target
  engagement in ALS patients confirmed in a Phase 1 trial, reported December 2025.
- **UNC13A program** — targeting the cryptic-exon mis-splicing of UNC13A pre-mRNA caused by loss of
  TDP-43 function in ALS and FTD.

Beyond ALS, the company states it is expanding into epilepsy, pain, frontotemporal dementia,
Fragile X syndrome and progressive supranuclear palsy.

## Platform

- **FlexASO®** — QurAlis's proprietary anti-sense oligonucleotide splice-modulator platform, used
  to address the spectrum of neurodegenerative and neurological diseases.
